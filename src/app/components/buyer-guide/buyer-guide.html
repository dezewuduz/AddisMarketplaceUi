Buyer Guide

How to Buy

No registration, no password — just 4 simple steps.

1

Pick a Product

Use a category (chip or map) to find what you want, or type a name in the "Search" box.

2

Click "Buy"

Every product card has a "Buy" button. Clicking it opens a short form.

3

Give Your Name and Phone Number

No password or registration needed — just fill in your name, phone number, and the quantity you want.

4

The Seller Will Call You

Your order reaches the seller right away. The seller will call you to arrange delivery and payment (in person or by agreement).
